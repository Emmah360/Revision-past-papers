Quick Facts

  • Forest Cover Target: Kenya aims to plant 15 billion trees by 2032.
  • Renewable Energy: Over 85% of Kenya's electricity comes from geothermal, hydro, wind, and solar power.
  • Rising Temperatures: East Africa faces increasingly erratic rainfall and prolonged droughts.
  • Youth Action: Local community tree nurseries restore degraded watersheds.

Protecting Our Environment for Future Generations

Climate change poses a direct threat to agriculture, water security, and biodiversity across Kenya. From melting glaciers on Mount Kenya to recurring droughts in arid and semi-arid lands, the impact of global warming is already visible in everyday livelihoods.
