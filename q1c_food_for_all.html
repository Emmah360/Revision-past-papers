Food for All Initiative - Nairobi

Food for All Initiative is a Nairobi-based non-governmental organization dedicated to eradicating hunger and fighting food insecurity across vulnerable urban settlements and rural communities in Kenya through sustainable food distribution and agricultural empowerment.

Our Core Programmes

  • School Feeding Programme for Informal Settlements
  • Urban Kitchen Gardens and Vertical Farming Training
  • Emergency Relief Food Distribution Network
